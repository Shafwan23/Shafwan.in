The brief

At SUMZ Technologies the work was ServiceNow platform development across IT Service Management and HR Service Delivery: building and customising modules so the platform fits each client's business processes and enforces their governance, for fourteen months and more than one client.

The build

ITSM modules, Incident, Problem and Change Management, configured and customised to the client's workflows. HR Service Delivery that extends case management to HR processes. Underneath, the platform logic: Client Scripts, Business Rules, Script Includes, UI Policies and ACLs that keep data correct and visible only to the right people.

Service requests automated with Flow Designer and the Workflow Editor, cutting manual intervention and turnaround time. Service Catalog items and Service Portal pages for the people raising the requests; SLAs and scheduled jobs behind the promises; REST and JDBC integrations to the systems around the platform; the Automated Test Framework so a change to one flow could not quietly break another.

The lesson

Working that close to business process changed how I approach problems. A technically correct solution still fails if it doesn't fit how people actually work.
