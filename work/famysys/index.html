The brief

Famysys makes products for clients and for itself, and since November 2025 I have been the engineer who ships them. The brief is always the same shape: take a business need to production, from the first screen to the database under it to the deploy that puts it in front of people, and keep it running afterwards.

The build

Seven products in eleven months, each a different kind of thing. Two Next.js sites, three React apps, a dependency-free dashboard that reads a CRM's API, and one internal platform with four Express services behind it. The ledger on the right is the record; the two private ones have cases of their own.

The practice

Delivery is owned across the full cycle: development, testing, Git, deployment, debugging and production support. The part that matters most is the whole path, from a requirement on a page to something running reliably in production, and then the next one.
