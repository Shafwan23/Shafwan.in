Case Study — Smart India Hackathon

SIH 2023 SIH 2023

Category
Hackathon · explainable AI on satellite data
Role
Team member, model and web app · Grand Finalist, Dec 2023
Stack
Python · Random Forest · SVM · CNN · SHAP · LIME · JavaScript

The brief

The Smart India Hackathon is a national competition where student teams build solutions to problems posed by ministries and industry. Our college team took ISRO problem statement 1521: an explainable AI model that predicts heavy and high-impact rain events from INSAT-3DR satellite data, so that a forecast comes with its reasons.

The approach

Precipitation estimates from INSAT-3DR, cleaned and pre-processed for learning, combined with weather forecasts and historical data. Three models, Random Forest, Support Vector Machine and a deep Convolutional Neural Network, classify each cell and window as high-impact or not, and vote.

The point was trust. Each prediction carries an explanation from SHAP and LIME, which signals pushed it over the line and by how much, delivered through a web application in Python and JavaScript. By submission the product was about 60% built, with testing and the interface still ahead.

The result

Grand Finalist, Smart India Hackathon 2023, issued by ISRO, Department of Space, in December 2023, with a certificate and memento from the Grand Finale.

The pipeline

  1. ObserveINSAT-3DR precipitation on a 0.1° grid
  2. PrepareClean, window, join forecasts and history
  3. ModelRandom ForestSVMCNN
  4. DecideHigh-impact or not, by vote
  5. ExplainSHAPLIME
  6. DeliverA web app in Python and JavaScript
