Three convictions

02 — How I work

  1. I

    A technically correct solution still fails if it doesn’t fit how people actually work.

    I spent over a year as a ServiceNow Developer, delivering ITSM and HR Service Delivery: configuring Incident, Problem and Change Management, automating service requests with Flow Designer, building Service Catalog and Service Portal experiences. Working that close to business process changed how I approach problems.

    Where I learned itServiceNow at SUMZ
  2. II

    The job is the whole path, from requirement to production.

    Interface, API, data layer, tests, deployment, and the support after launch. I work across all of it because a feature is only finished when it is running reliably in front of the people it was built for.

    Where it showsFamysys EMS
  3. III

    AI belongs inside the system, not bolted on top.

    LLMs and retrieval-augmented generation earn their place when they are part of how a system works: fed by its data, bound by its permissions, measured like any other service. Added on top as a chat box, they are a demo. That is the direction I am building toward.
